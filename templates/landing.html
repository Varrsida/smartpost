About SmartPost

SmartPost is a modern, web-based Post Office Management System built to digitize postal operations, streamline customer bookings, automate postage calculations, and deliver artificial intelligence insights into shipment delays.

By combining reliable database storage (SQLite) with machine learning algorithms (Random Forest), SmartPost empowers postal administrators and counter clerks to predict delivery risks in real time, minimize bottleneck delays, and maintain transparent audit tracking for every parcel.

How It Works

6 simple steps from customer registration to final doorstep delivery.

Step 1

Customer Registration

Register sender details with valid government ID proof.

Step 2

Postal Booking

Select service type, enter package weight, and declare item value.

Step 3

Tracking Generation

Python dynamically issues a unique tracking ID (e.g. SP202608160001).

Step 4

Shipment Processing

Dispatch package through regional sorting hubs and transit depots.

Step 5

Real-Time Tracking

Monitor vertical timeline status updates and AI delay alerts.

Step 6

Successful Delivery

Final recipient confirmation, digital signature, and closure.
